# B088: source review

Static surrogate plus constrained genetic search, not a learned recurrent world model. Patient geometry is held fixed while antenna position/direction, power and duration vary. Numerical training labels use Helmholtz FEM, Pennes FDM and Arrhenius damage. Prediction test Dice 95.06% and IoU 90.78% are simulation agreement. GA uses population 40, 60 generations, elitism 4, tournament 3, mutation rate 0.25/scale 0.1. Mean predicted AE 0.764 versus 0.495, CP 1.000 versus 0.998, OD 0.175 versus 0.389 and insertion length 71.2 versus 73.6 mm across 13 cases. Two clinicians judged trajectories feasible; one time/power setting indeterminate. Inference 16.3 ms and planning 42.7 s on RTX3090/Ryzen5900X, versus extrapolated numerical planning 17,971 s. No prospective or treated-patient outcome claim is established.

## Limitations

- Surrogate labels and compared plan outcomes come from simulations/the same learned surrogate, not observed ablation outcomes. Human CT geometry does not convert simulated lesion labels into clinical predictive validation.
- The roughly 420-fold numerical-planning comparison is extrapolated from 1,664 forward evaluations and a measured per-simulation time; no full numerical GA run is reported.
- Expert feasibility ratings are not evidence of improved patient outcomes, safety or actual treatment delivery.
- Code URL is explicitly reported but was not accessed; no weights/license/reproduction verified.
- Single Codex source review; no independent human review, external source retrieval, code execution/replication or prospective clinical validation performed by reviewer.
- All main-PDF pages including references/appendices read; every identified figure/table inspected. Referenced studies are bibliography only and do not transfer capabilities.
- Mechanical schema/source/coverage checks establish necessary conditions and quote occurrence, not scientific truth or clinical safety.
