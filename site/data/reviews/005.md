## Architecture and classification

The specified predictor maps an imputed, encoded and scaled tabular feature snapshot through RF/XGBoost base learners and an LR meta-learner to an in-hospital mortality probability, with SHAP explanations. The paper explicitly acknowledges on p. 23 that the current model uses static features and leaves temporal encoders to future work. No persistent world state, patient-state transition or settable intervention simulator is implemented. RPO searches hyperparameters; it is not a clinical world-model controller. Medication fields are observations, not intervention queries. Capability is `not_world_model`; the legacy static-predictor exclusion is retained.

BP_COMPONENT indicates a possible component-level analysis (risk readout/probability scoring), not inclusion as a world model. Six reported comparison artifacts are represented; no ghost DNN implementation is invented from its isolated mention. No healthcare world type is manufactured from the disease domain alone.

No R4–R8 is assigned. Retrospective case descriptions and a claimed dashboard are not a prospective human study. ## Source issues retained

Ten queue items, RQ-005-001 through 010, cover:

- Inconsistent cohort/site/feature descriptions: 1000 final patients, 3000 screened, and a separate 45,000-encounter/128-feature optimization paragraph versus 475 features elsewhere.
- A mortality-versus-LOS target switch; CleanEHR's instruction to discard expired hospitalizations and undefined core-field symbols.
- Admission-time use versus first-six-hour measurements, plus incomplete fit/timing and selected external-cohort details.
- Conflicting AUROC/SHAP numbers and incorrect or ambiguous figure descriptions. Figure 8 is correlation-like rather than the captioned class-wise SHAP plot; Figure 7 labels TP as survivors plus deceased; Figure 3 is an AUROC/Brier comparison.
- Zero imputation in Figure 2 versus mean/mode imputation in Methods, and a Figure 1 legend/prose variable mismatch.
- RPO bounds excluding the stated RF depth optimum; unsubstantiated DNN configuration and fold-level balancing questions.
- Unsupported upgrades from Brier/SHAP to clinical assurances and inconsistent data-repository/reference descriptions.

The confusion matrix itself is numerically coherent with rounded accuracy/precision/recall/F1. That does not reconcile the AUROC, cohort or feature-selection issues. Source problems are flagged without asserting fabrication.
