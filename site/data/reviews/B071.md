## Limitations

- Clinical datasets are independently fit domains, not external held-out deployment transfer; 50 generated cohorts do not measure model refit variability.
- No prospective care study, action-conditioned causal effect identification or model-coupled planner. VELOUR trial provenance does not make this generator a randomized treatment-effect evaluation.
- TSTR MSE belongs to a separate S4 model and does not validate generator conditional future prediction.
- Clinical C2ST AUROC 0.998 VELOUR and 0.996 PPMI means generated patients remain easily distinguishable. Strong selected MMD results do not establish clinical realism.
- Posterior sampling pools approximate static posteriors; patient correspondence is discarded, but that alone is not a privacy mechanism. No differential privacy guarantee.
- Code release and restricted data access are stated in PDF, not externally verified.
- Single Codex source review; no independent human review, external source retrieval, code execution/replication or prospective clinical validation performed by reviewer.
- All main-PDF pages including references/appendices read; every identified figure/table inspected. Referenced studies are bibliography only and do not transfer capabilities.
- Mechanical schema/source/coverage checks establish necessary conditions and quote occurrence, not scientific truth or clinical safety.
