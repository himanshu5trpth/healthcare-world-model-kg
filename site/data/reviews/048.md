# 048 source review
All 18 physical pages were read, including references on pages 10–11 and figure-only pages 11–18. All eight figures and the unnumbered comparative table on page 7 were visually inspected. Version: Research Square v1, 21 April 2025, DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-6449815/v1.

The contribution is an asserted HDP/decentralized mutual federated binary heart-disease classifier. It is not a world model: absence of longitudinal data is explicit (page 8), and temporal ECG/recurrent models are future work (page 7). Static HDP latent clusters and training rounds do not establish patient dynamics. Legacy exclusion and not-world-model values are retained.

Scientific reporting is seriously unresolved. The frontmatter calls this a systematic review, methods describe benchmark simulations, and results suddenly report 1427 screened records, twelve studies and a network meta-analysis. Underlying included-study extraction and complete algorithm are absent; the supplement is referenced but not supplied. Rival results are preserved separately in structured model properties: abstract accuracy94.2/F1 93.2/AUC.97; discussion accuracy96.4/F1 95.7/AUC.98; table combined accuracy92/precision91/recall83; later prose accuracy92/precision89/recall87/F1 88. Meta-analysis figures and effect labels have different unresolved scopes.

The visual audit finds additional problems missed by text extraction. Figure 8 shows global accuracy.9086 and class1 recall.13/F1.21; client precision, recall and F1 averages exceed1, making them invalid as conventional unit-scale metrics. Figure 1 labels accuracy61.77% but count/colour scales conflict; Figures2–3 have inconsistent counts/colourbars/metric titles; Figure6 is captioned F1 but shows support proportions. Training Figure4 is labelled decision-tree loss rather than the proposed network. These are source errors/ambiguities, not reviewer-corrected results. No best value is selected or pooled.

R1 simulation/technical evidence is recorded with severe provenance limits, not R2 verified patient-cohort validation. Local data retention is a design intention; concrete privacy protocol and attack test are not established. Code is promised after publication, not released. All 182 raw legacy values remain unchanged; legacy FN abstract metrics require the new conflicting-scope qualification. Reviewer synthesis: no reuse recommendation before dataset, implementation and metric provenance are independently resolved.
