All 20 physical PDF pages were read, including references on pages 14–15. Three figures and two tables on pages 16–20 were visually inspected. The supplied source is medRxiv, 11 August 2025.

GenoMed4All is supporting federated infrastructure, not a world model. Local FHIR ETL datasets feed task-specific DeepSurv/SAVAE survival predictors or CTGAN/BN/VAE-BGM static tabular generators. Manager/worker orchestration uses Flower/FedAvg. WSI Prov-Gigapath embeddings and PET radiomics are used with clinical/genomic variables. Training rounds and static VAE latent variables never establish evolving health state. Operational security includes TLS, key exchange, OpenID/Keycloak and RBAC; these are concrete mechanisms, but no universal privacy guarantee follows.

Cohorts: MDS 4427, AML 1547, CMML 1737, MM 1005, with MDS WSI subgroup 860. A random 90/10 split precedes uneven three/five worker partitions; therefore results are retrospective internal validation with fragmented-feature/node-size robustness tests, not heldout external institutions. All principal Table 2 C-indices are preserved as structured raw_table2_results on the platform entity with model/cohort, feature scenario, fractions and unresolved split scope. For example, CMML best-case centralized .69, FL nodes .712/.692/.688, isolated smallest .636, conventional CPSS-mol .62. MDS with WSI best-case centralized .797, FL .767/.785/.785; worst-case node2 .710. Results are heterogeneous, not universal improvement. Figure 2 caption swaps node2/3 fractions relative to legend/Table 2. Figure 3 is a CSF/GSF composite curve, not separate fully reported metrics; no exact values were digitized.

SAFE evaluates synthetic statistical/clinical fidelity and NNDR, but nearest-neighbor distance alone cannot rule out membership or reidentification risk. Synthetic survival agreement and nonsignificant comparisons are not proof of clinical equivalence. Five installed worker nodes and two managers establish infrastructure deployment, not prospective patient treatment. Prospective model validation is future work (page 13). Trial registration and ethics approval are documented, not regulatory authorization. Legacy component/not-world-model/R2 classifications retained, with all 182 raw values preserved. Reviewer synthesis: reuse site-local adapters and security responsibilities with new composition-specific provenance, privacy and clinical validation.
