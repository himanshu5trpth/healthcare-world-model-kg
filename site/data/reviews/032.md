# 032 source review
All 34 physical PDF pages were read, including references on pages 26–34. All four figures and two tables were visually inspected, together with regression Equations 4–5. The supplied version is the medRxiv preprint posted 19 September 2024.

This is a supporting conformal prediction component, not a world model. Its own experiments fit three static configurations: SVM with TCP for infliximab response (44 Crohn's disease and 34 rheumatoid arthritis patients, 16 test samples); XGBoost with ICP for DLBCL subtypes (GSE181063, plus transfer to 789 GSE117556 samples); and RF with ICP for afatinib IC50 (765 cell lines reduced to 677, 20% test, calibration on 20% of training). Cited imaging, variant, vaccine and time-series systems are other work. A 72-hour assay endpoint and TCP model refitting do not establish health-world transitions.

Table 1 (page 21) reports binary coverage 93.75%, error 6.25%, and uncertain rate 12.5% at nominal 95%; page 17 instead says 2.25% error. Internal DLBCL coverage is 95.2% with 35.6% uncertain outputs. External Table 1 coverage is 96.7% with 38.18% uncertain outputs; prose reports 96.6%. External MMD is .0011 with permutation p=.017. Dataset shift is tested, but overlap, independence and calibration handling are insufficiently pinned to verify R3. Table 2 gives nominal .90, residual cutoff 6.77 and coverage 92.6%; nominal .95, cutoff 6.98 and coverage 95.5% (prose 95.6%). Preserve table values and numerical disagreements.

Equation 4 prints a quantile level as though it were a residual value used by Equation 5. Record this source notation issue without inventing an algorithm correction. Page 24 correctly limits guarantees to marginal coverage and denies individual or subgroup guarantees. Page 25 nevertheless says a singleton mathematically guarantees a safe decision; this is an unsupported clinical safety claim.

Verified readiness is R2 for the internal retrospective human classifiers and R1 for cell-line data analysis. Expert referral is proposed; staff availability, response timing and fallback are unimplemented. No reader study, prospective care use or patient benefit is shown. All 182 raw legacy cells remain unchanged. The ET R3 label is qualified as internal validation plus external transfer with an independence gap. Reviewer synthesis: reuse requires new trajectory/action-regime calibration; static marginal coverage cannot certify a world model or clinical safety.
