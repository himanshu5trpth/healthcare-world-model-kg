# B082: source review

S2013 extends a continuous physiological simulator with nonlinear hypoglycemic insulin utilization and glucagon secretion/action/transport. Compartments define interpretable glucose/insulin/glucagon state and physiological action channels. Clinical clamp minimal-model parameters fit before a60mg/dl threshold predict after it, deliberately underestimating glucose due to missing counterregulation; this is not a calibrated fullsimulator success result. Glucagon transport assessed using11 adults and13 datasets from27-hour bihormonal studies, with acknowledged confounding by changing insulin/repeated dosing. Virtual subjects100 adults/100 adolescents/100children draw constrained parameter vectors. Simulated50g meal at08:00 with patient-specific bolus demonstrates trajectories; controller box in Figure1 is interface capability, not evaluated closed-loop controller in this paper. FDA single-meal statement is narrowly scoped and unverified externally.

## Limitations

- Main S2013 results are single-meal open-loop simulations, not prospective controlled patient benefit.
- Clinical component fitting/prediction is not held-out clinical validation of the entire integrated S2013 simulator. The 32-person clamp prediction intentionally underestimates later glucose to expose missing counterregulation.
- Virtual-patient random variation is intersubject parameter uncertainty, not a random transition law or calibrated predictive posterior.
- FDA acceptance is an author-reported preclinical single-meal simulation context, not therapeutic device authorization; version/date applicability unresolved from submitted/accepted wording.
- Appendix equations are available but mathematical PDF extraction has font/order artifacts; visual source inspection used for physiological interpretation, no equations executed.
- Single Codex source review; no independent human review, external source retrieval, code execution/replication or prospective clinical validation performed by reviewer.
- All main-PDF pages including references/appendices read; every identified figure/table inspected. Referenced studies are bibliography only and do not transfer capabilities.
- Mechanical schema/source/coverage checks establish necessary conditions and quote occurrence, not scientific truth or clinical safety.
