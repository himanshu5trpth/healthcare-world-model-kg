Engineering verification framework with three axes, four fidelity layers, first-crossing effective-horizon procedure and a purposive nine-model reporting audit. The worked horizon example digitizes published curves; it is not a new patient-level evaluation or a new trained world model.

Every physical PDF page, including references and appendices, read. All numbered figures and tables directly inspected from rendered source pages. References were read as bibliographic entries, not retrieved as additional annotated studies.

## Limits

- All14physical pages including references read; no original patient data collected.
- Nine-model purposive selection and single-author coding; no independent duplicate extraction and no exhaustive prevalence claim.
- Digitized illustrative curves are approximate; no patient-level confidence intervals or validated action-support horizon is obtained.
- No functional capability or clinical readiness is inherited from the nine audited models.
- One automated Codex source-review pass with skeptical criterion check; no independent human review or experimental reproduction.
- Source references/bibliography were read as entries, not retrieved as independent primary studies.
- Schema/quote occurrence checks are necessary checks, not proof of clinical safety, causal identification or complete scientific correctness.
