## Limitations

- Clinical images supply anatomy/initialization only; no actual longitudinal human target establishes R2.
- Chemotherapy uses abstract action units and a simplified toxicity state; these are not clinical executable doses or adverse events.
- Robust MPC covers only±0.001multiplicative state perturbations, not parameter/treatment-response/imaging uncertainty.
- Dense/sparse configurations use different evaluation timepoints; effects cannot be attributed solely to observation frequency.
- Reference simulator shares treatment equations and overall RD structure with controller; model mismatch is controlled spatial proliferation, limiting generalization.
- Single Codex source review; no independent human review, external source retrieval, code execution/replication or prospective clinical validation performed by reviewer.
- All main-PDF pages including references/appendices read; every identified figure/table inspected. Referenced studies are bibliography only and do not transfer capabilities.
- Mechanical schema/source/coverage checks establish necessary conditions and quote occurrence, not scientific truth or clinical safety.
