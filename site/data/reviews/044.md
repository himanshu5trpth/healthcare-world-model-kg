# 044 source review
All 15 physical PDF pages were read, including references on pages 10–12 and appendices on pages 13–15. Eight figures, two tables and Algorithm 1 were visually inspected. Version: arXiv 2510.20952v1, 23 October 2025.

LBS qualifies as a core autonomous-dynamics healthcare world model at L1 for its TTC-MEDICAL configuration. A 16-dimensional GRU Gaussian prior advances daily clinical state; current heart-rate value and LoRA LLaMA3.1-8B summary tokens update a Gaussian posterior. The shared latent feeds numeric MLP and text decoder. Stateful single-step ELBO training reconstructs observations with KL regularization; free-nats annealing is specified. Treatment references in notes are observed context, not settable actions. No intervention, causal effect or model-coupled planner is demonstrated.

The medical benchmark has 73 hospitalized patients, averaging 104 days, with chronological 80/10/10 train/validation/test splits. Table 2 (page 15) medical RMSE arrays for every method/horizon are preserved as structured model properties. Multimodal LBS is 3.583 at H1 and 5.487 at H6; unimodal 3.910 and 5.654. Units/normalization and uncertainty intervals are unreported. Table 1 medical aggregate 4.733 versus 4.992 and 5.410% improvement are retained separately as printed, without recalculation. Medical H6 is tested extent; climate H7 is another scope. Multiple horizons and autoregressive latent architecture are present, but exact test-loop replacement of intermediate observations is not specified sufficiently to certify free-running rollout reliability or an effective clinical horizon.

Uncertainty variance from ten prior samples, seasonality t-SNE and generated-text examples are all climate analyses (pages 7–8), not clinical calibration or medical text-fidelity evidence. No nominal coverage, external clinical validation or actual clinician use is reported. Broader impact recognizes plausible but misleading text and recommends oversight, without an implemented safeguard. Larger-LLM scaling does not consistently help on climate and does not establish clinical transfer. Legacy core/L1/R2 retained; raw 182 cells preserved. Legacy AE/CF Yes are qualified as partial because recursive/error-accumulation protocol remains unclear. Reviewer synthesis: promising state/belief and emission contract, requiring clinical availability/leakage audit, trajectory calibration and independent deployment validation before care use.
