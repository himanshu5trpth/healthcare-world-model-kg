# 043 source review
All eight physical PDF pages were read, including references on pages 7–8. All ten figures and Table I were visually inspected. The source header is Journal of Robotics and Control, November 2022, DOI 10.18196/jrc.v3i6.16200; legacy year 2022 is correct despite filename 2023.

The paper reports a static cell-image classifier, not treatment simulation or health dynamics. Convolution/pooling/dense processing, Bayesian weight-prior language and possible pretrained ResNet50 variants are described. Culture days define early/late labels rather than propagated state. No individualized intervention comparison, causal effect, planner or patient outcome study is demonstrated.

Table I gives DBNN accuracy 98.73%, CNN 89.40% and SVM 79.58%; the latter two are attributed to reference 28 rather than clearly matched reruns. These raw findings are structured on the model entity with unknown cohort/split scope. Page 5 also reports around 96% independent test and 97% validation accuracy for a TensorFlow variant, with unresolved run identity. Methods cite C-NMC but the narrative alternates binary leukemia, five leukocyte classes and early/late culture differentiation. Figure 10 actually has eight classes, including erythroblast, immature granulocyte and platelet. Sample/patient counts and label provenance are not reconciled. The 7:2:1 split does not document patient/slide separation. Weight-prior/posterior inference details and empirical uncertainty calibration are insufficient.

Legacy excluded/static/not-world-model values retained. R1 technical classification is supported; a separately attributable retrospective human R2 cohort is unresolved. No human-superiority or personalized-treatment claim is promoted. All 182 raw legacy cells remain unchanged. Reviewer synthesis: classifier evidence would require repaired dataset/run provenance and independent verification before reuse as a clinical encoder or uncertainty component.
